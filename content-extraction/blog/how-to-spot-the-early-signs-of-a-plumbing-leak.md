# How to Spot the Early Signs of a Plumbing Leak

Plumbing leaks can cause significant damage if left unchecked, and they often start small, making them easy to miss. Spotting the early signs of a plumbing leak can save you from costly repairs and preserve the integrity of your home. At Courtesy Plumbing & Heating in Castle Rock, Colorado, we've seen how catching these leaks early can make all the difference. Here's what to watch for to keep your home safe and dry.

## 1. Unexplained Increase in Water Bills

A sudden spike in your water bill is one of the most common signs of a hidden leak. If your usage habits haven't changed, but your bills have, it's time to investigate. Small drips can waste gallons of water over time, and in Castle Rock's semi-arid climate, water conservation is especially crucial.

## 2. Low Water Pressure

If your faucets or showerheads have weaker water pressure than usual, a leak might be diverting water elsewhere. While this can sometimes indicate a clogged pipe, it's worth having a professional check for hidden leaks.

## 3. Water Stains and Discoloration

Look for yellow or brown stains on walls, ceilings, or floors. These often indicate water seeping from a pipe behind the surface. Over time, the moisture can lead to structural damage and mold growth, creating even bigger problems.

## 4. Unusual Sounds

Do you hear dripping, hissing, or running water when no taps are on? These noises could signal a leak within your walls or under your floors. Don't ignore these sounds—leaks can worsen quickly if not addressed.

## 5. Musty or Damp Smells

A persistent musty odor in your home can indicate moisture from a hidden leak. Mold thrives in damp environments, and even a small leak can create the perfect conditions for growth. If you smell something off, it's worth investigating further.

## 6. Visible Mold or Mildew

Mold and mildew require moisture to grow, and their appearance often points to a leak. In Colorado's dry climate, excessive moisture is unusual, so seeing mold on walls, floors, or ceilings could mean there's a hidden plumbing issue.

## 7. Cracks in Your Foundation or Walls

Leaks in underground pipes can cause shifts in the soil, leading to cracks in your foundation or walls. These structural issues can be costly to repair, making it critical to address leaks promptly.

## 8. Wet Spots or Pooling Water

Unexplained wet spots on your lawn or in your home might indicate a plumbing issue. Outdoors, these spots could suggest a broken underground pipe, while indoor puddles may mean a burst or leaking pipe.

## What to Do If You Suspect a Leak

If you notice any of these signs, don't wait to address the issue. A small leak can quickly escalate into a major problem. At Courtesy Plumbing & Heating, we specialize in leak detection and repair for Castle Rock residents. Our team uses advanced tools to locate and fix leaks efficiently, saving you time and money.

### Preventative Tips

- **Monitor Your Water Meter:** Turn off all water in your home and check the meter. If it continues to move, you likely have a leak.
- **Inspect Exposed Pipes:** Regularly check under sinks and around your water heater for visible leaks.
- **Schedule Regular Maintenance:** Routine plumbing inspections can catch issues before they become emergencies.
