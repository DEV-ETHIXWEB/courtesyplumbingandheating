## Understanding Hydro Jetting:

Hydro Jetting is a cutting-edge method used to clean and clear sewer lines by employing high-pressure water streams. Unlike traditional snaking methods that merely bore through clogs, Hydro Jetting utilizes the force of water to thoroughly scour and flush away debris, grease, roots, and other stubborn obstructions clinging to the inner walls of sewer pipes.

## The Hydro Jetting Process:

1. **Assessment:** Before commencing the Hydro Jetting process, our team conducts a thorough inspection of your sewer lines using state-of-the-art camera technology. This allows us to pinpoint the location and nature of any blockages or buildup within the pipes.
2. **Preparation:** With the assessment complete, our certified technicians prepare the Hydro Jetting equipment, including specialized nozzles attached to flexible hoses and a high-pressure water pump. The system is calibrated to deliver precise water pressure tailored to the specific needs of your sewer lines.
3. **Execution:** Once the equipment is in place, the Hydro Jetting process begins. The powerful streams of water are directed into the sewer lines, effectively dislodging and disintegrating any debris or obstructions in their path. The force of the water can reach up to 4,000 PSI, ensuring thorough cleaning and restoration of optimal flow.
4. **Inspection:** Following the Hydro Jetting procedure, we conduct a secondary camera inspection to verify the success of the operation. Our technicians ensure that all debris and buildup have been effectively removed, leaving your sewer lines pristine and free-flowing.

## The Benefits of Hydro Jetting:

- **Effective Cleaning:** Hydro Jetting provides a comprehensive cleaning solution that eliminates even the most stubborn blockages, including grease, tree roots, scale, and sediment.
- **Preventive Maintenance:** Regular Hydro Jetting helps prevent future clogs and backups by thoroughly clearing debris and buildup from sewer lines, extending their lifespan and minimizing the need for costly repairs.
- **Environmentally Friendly:** Hydro Jetting is a chemical-free and environmentally friendly method of sewer line cleaning, relying solely on the power of water to achieve exceptional results.
- **Cost-Effective:** While initial Hydro Jetting may seem like an investment, its long-term benefits far outweigh the costs by reducing the frequency of emergency plumbing calls and prolonging the lifespan of sewer lines.
