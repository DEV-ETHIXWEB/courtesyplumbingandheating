Winter in Colorado can be breathtaking, but it also brings freezing temperatures that can wreak havoc on your home's plumbing. At Courtesy Plumbing & Heating, we understand how essential it is to keep your plumbing in top shape to avoid the headaches of unexpected repairs. As your trusted plumber in Castle Rock, we've compiled these key seasonal plumbing tips to help you safeguard your home during the harsh winter months.

#### 1. Insulate Your Pipes

One of the most effective ways to prevent pipes from freezing and bursting is by insulating them. Focus on exposed pipes in unheated areas such as basements, attics, garages, and crawl spaces. Using foam pipe insulation or heat tape can provide an extra layer of protection against freezing temperatures. If you're unsure of where to start, a plumber in Castle Rock can assess your home and recommend the best insulation solutions.

#### 2. Maintain a Steady Thermostat Temperature

To minimize the risk of frozen pipes, keep your thermostat set to a consistent temperature throughout the day and night. While it might be tempting to lower the heat at night to save on energy, maintaining a stable temperature will help ensure that your plumbing stays protected. This small step can prevent costly repairs down the line.

#### 3. Disconnect and Drain Outdoor Hoses

Outdoor hoses can trap water that freezes and backs up into your plumbing system, causing pipes to burst. Always disconnect and store your garden hoses before the first frost. Additionally, shut off the water supply to outdoor faucets and drain any remaining water from them to prevent freezing.

#### 4. Seal Any Drafts

Cold air can seep into your home through gaps and cracks near windows, doors, and other openings, potentially exposing pipes to freezing temperatures. Seal these drafts with caulk or weather stripping to maintain warmth and protect vulnerable plumbing areas. Consulting a plumber in Castle Rock can help you identify hidden spots that may require extra attention.

#### 5. Leave Cabinet Doors Open

Pipes located under sinks or in cabinets are more susceptible to freezing if they are in unheated spaces. By leaving cabinet doors open, you allow warm air from your home to circulate and reach the pipes. This simple step can prevent blockages and bursts when temperatures dip.

#### 6. Let Faucets Drip

When temperatures are expected to drop significantly, leaving a faucet dripping slightly can relieve pressure in the plumbing system and reduce the chances of pipes freezing. This is especially important for faucets connected to pipes that run along exterior walls.

#### 7. Know How to Shut Off Your Water

In the event of a plumbing emergency, knowing how to shut off your main water valve can save you from significant water damage. Make sure you and your family members are familiar with the valve's location and how to turn it off quickly. If you're unsure, reach out to Courtesy Plumbing & Heating for guidance.
