Your home's plumbing system is one of its most vital components, ensuring clean water flows in and wastewater flows out. Despite its importance, many homeowners aren't familiar with how their plumbing works or how to identify potential issues before they escalate.

At Courtesy Plumbing & Heating, we believe an informed homeowner is an empowered one. That's why we've put together this beginner's guide to help you better understand your home's plumbing system and keep it running smoothly.

## The Basics of Your Plumbing System

### 1. Water Supply System

The water supply system delivers clean water to your home. This water typically comes from either a municipal water supply or a private well.

**Key Components:**
- **Main water line:** Connects your home to the water source.
- **Shut-off valve:** Allows you to turn off the water supply in case of an emergency.
- **Pipes and fixtures:** Deliver water to faucets, appliances, and other outlets.

**Tip:** Locate your main shut-off valve - it's crucial in the event of a leak or burst pipe.

### 2. Drainage System

The drainage system removes wastewater from your home and directs it to a municipal sewer or septic system.

**Key Components:**
- **Drainpipes:** Carry wastewater from sinks, toilets, and appliances.
- **Traps:** U-shaped pipes under sinks that prevent sewer gases from entering your home.
- **Vents:** Pipes that allow air to circulate and ensure proper drainage flow.

**Tip:** Never pour grease or non-biodegradable items down your drains - they can clog your system.

### 3. Water Heater

Your water heater provides the hot water you use for bathing, washing dishes, and doing laundry.

**Types of Water Heaters:**
- **Tank Water Heaters:** Store and heat water in a tank.
- **Tankless Water Heaters:** Heat water on demand without storing it.

**Tip:** Flush your water heater annually to remove sediment buildup and maintain efficiency.

### 4. Fixtures and Appliances

From sinks and toilets to dishwashers and washing machines, plumbing fixtures and appliances rely on your system for water supply and drainage.

**Common Fixtures and Their Maintenance:**
- **Faucets:** Replace washers or cartridges to stop drips.
- **Toilets:** Check for leaks by adding food coloring to the tank; if color appears in the bowl, there's a leak.
- **Showers:** Clean showerheads regularly to prevent mineral buildup.

**Tip:** Inspect fixtures for leaks regularly to prevent water waste and damage.

## Common Plumbing Problems and Solutions

### 1. Clogged Drains

Hair, grease, and debris can block drains over time, causing slow drainage or backups.

**Solution:** Use a plunger or drain snake to clear minor clogs. For persistent issues, call a professional.

### 2. Leaky Faucets and Pipes

Even a small leak can waste gallons of water and increase your water bill.

**Solution:** Replace faulty washers, seals, or pipes.

### 3. Low Water Pressure

Mineral deposits or leaks can reduce water flow from your faucets or showerheads.

**Solution:** Clean aerators and showerheads or check for hidden leaks.

### 4. Running Toilets

A continuously running toilet often signals a problem with the flapper or fill valve.

**Solution:** Adjust or replace the faulty component.

## When to Call a Professional

While many plumbing issues can be addressed with DIY solutions, some problems require professional expertise. Contact a plumber if you notice:
- Persistent clogs or backups.
- Sudden spikes in your water bill.
- Water stains or damp spots on walls or ceilings.
- Strange noises from your pipes or water heater.

## Preventative Plumbing Tips

1. **Insulate Pipes:** Prevent frozen pipes in winter by insulating exposed pipes.
2. **Install Strainers:** Use strainers in sinks and showers to catch debris and prevent clogs.
3. **Schedule Inspections:** Regular plumbing inspections can catch minor issues before they turn into costly repairs.
